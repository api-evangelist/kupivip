# KupiVIP

> KupiVIP (kupivip.ru) is a Russian online outlet retailer for luxury and premium
> fashion — women's and men's apparel, footwear, bags, accessories and home goods
> from 250+ international designer brands, sold at discounts of up to 90% off
> previous-season collections. The storefront is a consumer e-commerce site built
> on 1C-Bitrix; it ships across Russia, publishes an authenticity guarantee, and
> operates telephone, email, WhatsApp and Telegram customer support.
